04 / Process

From first message to live.

Eight clear steps. You always know where your change is, and nothing ships without your yes.

  1. You send the request: a line, a link, a Loom or a ticket. My first reply is questions, not a quote.

    Your briefMy questions

  2. I go through the store, theme and apps to check the change is possible, and find the cleanest way to do it.

    FeasibilityTheme & app check

  3. For a one-off project you get a clear estimate in hours and cost. On a long-term retainer there's no estimate: the work simply starts.

    Hours & costSkipped on retainer

  4. I collect anything still missing (designs, copy, images, store access) so nothing blocks the build later.

    FigmaContentAccess

  5. Every change is built on its own branch using my Git flow, or your team's flow if you already have one.

    Feature branchesYour flow or mine

  6. Tested on real phones, tablets and desktops, not just a resized browser, so it works for every customer.

    MobileTabletDesktop

  7. You review it on a preview theme and leave feedback. Any changes go back through the same flow.

    Preview linkYour comments

  8. Once you confirm the review, it goes live on your store. Nothing ships without your yes.

    Your approvalRelease

08 / FAQ

Good questions

Do you build custom themes or use pre-built ones?

Both. I build fully custom Shopify themes from scratch, or customise existing themes like Horizon, Dawn and Focal to match your brand and goals.

Can you improve my existing store?

Yes. I can improve performance, UI/UX and conversion rates, and add new features without rebuilding your store from scratch.

How long does a store build take?

It depends on complexity. A simple store can take 1–2 weeks; a fully custom build takes several weeks.

What do you work with?

Shopify Liquid, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus Shopify CLI, Git, Shopify Flow and the Shopify APIs.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes — a lot of my work is white-label theme development for European Shopify agencies, including two leading agencies in Germany.
